# Fidelity check against the finalized 30 requirements

Use this when checking a draft or revising the skill. Numbers preserve the original requirements for traceability; they prescribe neither paragraph order nor equal section lengths.

1. Discussion starts from an established contribution and its scholarly significance, beyond Findings retelling or Literature Review.
2. For the originating PhD, four connected dimensions form one situated design space for translating lived experience into participatory immersive form.
3. Reused participant material performs new argumentative work at the Discussion level.
4. Retain necessary lived context, bodily actions, spatial conditions, and participant language without replaying the whole Findings analysis.
5. Preserve analytical short quotations/keywords, usually 2–8 words; follow them with concrete interpretation.
6. Give each reused participant example a verified Findings section reference and final LaTeX label where applicable.
7. Use simple, direct sentences with conceptual richness; remove inflated academic packaging.
8. Complete each introduced thought with the relevant actor, action, reason, and consequence.
9. Every sentence carries necessary information or reasoning; remove empty transitions and filler.
10. Make sentence-to-sentence reasoning explicit; theory cannot supply missing empirical analysis.
11. Name concrete evidence instead of replacing known content with vague categories.
12. Explain immediately what particular/specific refers to, or remove the word.
13. Use clear, natural paragraph openings whose form follows their argumentative role.
14. Vary paragraph structure and length with the material; avoid four mechanically identical mini-essays.
15. State what cross-case comparison adds beyond separate examples.
16. Use theory as a dialogue partner to explain and sharpen an already identified relationship.
17. Keep the contribution in the active position and literature in a positioning role.
18. Add new literature selectively when it clarifies the study's further contribution.
19. Explain why each dimension bears its name at its first substantive introduction.
20. Name researcher-designer interpretation and action between participant input and immersive form.
21. Specify concrete design consequences in light, geometry, sound, action, access, or other supported features.
22. Give this/these and other pronouns unmistakable referents.
23. Avoid habitual artificial oppositions and rhetorical tension; express factual differences directly.
24. Avoid defensive disclaimers; state real limitations directly in their appropriate section.
25. Make clear, proportionate contributions without grandiose or universal claims.
26. Use I/my for an individual PhD's analysis and design decisions.
27. Explain what each dimension adds beyond its definition; do not turn the underlying questions into obligatory rhetorical questions.
28. End 6.1 by consolidating its existing contribution, without surprise frameworks or research questions.
29. Develop 6.2 naturally from individual immersive accounts to relations among memories in the monument.
30. In bilingual delivery, pair English thesis prose with natural Chinese checking paragraphs and preserve identical substantive claims.

Do not satisfy any item by inventing evidence. If a needed source, implementation record, exact quote, or label is unavailable, make that gap visible.
